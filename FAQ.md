# KIT•10 — FAQ

## What is KIT•10?

KIT•10 (pronounced **“kitten”**) is a framework and editor for building design systems from structured inputs instead of manually curated tokens and variants.

You don’t design *each* result.  
You define how the system behaves, and the results are derived.

---

## Is KIT•10 a design system?

No.

It’s a way to **generate** design systems.

Think “design system factory,” not “design system template.”

---

## Do I need to understand axes to use it?

At some point, yes — but not all at once.

Axes are just named controls for design intent (density, emphasis, contrast, etc.).  
The editor exists so you can explore what they do without memorizing theory first.

---

## How is this different from design tokens?

Tokens are outputs.

KIT•10 operates *before* tokens exist.  
It generates them by evaluating intent.

You can still ship tokens. You just don’t have to hand-maintain them.

---

## Is this replacing designers?

No.

It replaces repetitive decision-making, not judgment.

Designers still:
- define axes
- choose mappings
- set constraints
